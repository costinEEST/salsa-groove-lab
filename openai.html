How to use

Colored cells are instrument hits. Odd columns are downbeats; even columns are the “and” subdivisions. Solo overrides mute. Uploaded stems loop independently, while the row pattern remains your visual rhythm map.

  • Load separate audio stems for real instrument isolation.
  • Use demo synth mode when no stem is loaded.
  • Reset restores salsa defaults; Clear All empties the map.
  • Press Space to start or stop playback.
