How it works

Each instrument is synthesised live with the Web Audio API — no audio files required, so every track is genuinely isolated. You can also upload your own stems (drag a file onto any track's upload button) to study real recordings. The 16-step grid spans two bars of 4/4 — the full clave cycle. Dots mark downbeats; the heavier border separates the two measures.

The patterns

  • Clave — backbone: 2, 3, 5, &-of-6, 8 (2-3 Son); switchable to 3-2 Son or 2-3 Rumba
  • Cáscara — busy 8th-note pattern on the timbale shell
  • Campana — straight downbeats, the dance anchor
  • Bongo — continuous martillo through the verse
  • Conga tumbao — open tones on beats 4 and 8
  • Bass tumbao — anticipated on &-of-2 and beat 4 (root of next chord)
  • Piano montuno — syncopated guajeo with same anticipations: Dm ↔ G7
  • Horns — punchy mambo hits
  • Coro — response in bar 2 (the "call" is implied)
